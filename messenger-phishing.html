01
A login or code request is the tell
Being asked to re-log-in, "re-authenticate", verify an account, approve a push or hand over a 2FA code through a chat link is almost always the attack — in any channel, from any name.
02
A name is not proof
Display names, "CFO", even accounts with no external badge can be spoofed or compromised. Trust the request, not the label. Urgent + secret + skip-the-process is a pattern, not a coincidence.
03
Verify on a second channel
Anything unusual — money, data, access — gets confirmed through something you already control: a known number, a ticket you open, the IT channel you already use. Never the link in front of you.

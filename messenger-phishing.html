01
Login or password requests are the tell
Any message asking you to re-log-in, "verify your account", or enter a password through a link is almost always phishing — in any channel. Real systems don't chase you in chat.
02
IT never asks for codes or remote access
A 2FA code, a push approval, or remote control (Quick Assist, AnyDesk) is never a legitimate help-desk request. Treat that ask as the attack itself.
03
Verify through a second channel
"External" badges and unfamiliar work domains are reasons to slow down. Call the person, open a ticket yourself, message the IT channel you already know — never the link in front of you.
